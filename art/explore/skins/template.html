Quotient Quest skins

Here are 24 new skins in 4 rarity groups. Each skin has its own Health and Attack. Tap the skins you like, then copy the codes and send them to Claude.

Health plus Attack is 10 points for a common skin, 11 for rare and epic, and 12 for legendary. A legendary skin is a bit stronger, but each skin still has a weak side. These numbers are a first draft.
