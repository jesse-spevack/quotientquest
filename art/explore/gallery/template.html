Quotient Quest Skin Gallery

Here are __COUNT__ skins as real game sprites. Each skin moves in its idle loop. Tap a skin or a button to see its attack, its power attack, a hit, and a fall.

Each skin has a fight class. The class gives the skin its kind of moves. The game adds the glow, the arrows, and the magic in code, in the colors of each skin. Health and Attack are a first draft.
